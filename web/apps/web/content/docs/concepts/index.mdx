Amicu is declarative. You describe the stack in HCL, amicu compares it with recorded state, and `apply` makes the difference real.

- [Plan, apply and state](/docs/concepts/plan-apply-state): what `validate`, `plan`, and `apply` do, and what state records.
- [Resources and references](/docs/concepts/resources): the resource kinds and how they refer to each other.
- [Residency and regions](/docs/concepts/residency): how jurisdictions, providers, and regions are checked.

Related: [Secrets](/docs/security/secrets) explains how credentials stay out of configuration and state.
